The Christ's Church app helps you stay connected throughout the week.

Watch recent messages, save messages for later, discover upcoming events, add events to your calendar, and find simple next steps for visiting, connecting, serving, and giving.

Features:

- Watch recent Sunday messages
- Save messages to revisit later
- Browse upcoming church events
- Add events to your device calendar
- Schedule optional event reminders
- Find service times, directions, and contact details
- Fill out a connection card
- Explore ministries and serving opportunities
- Give through the church's secure giving partner
- Read saved public content when offline

Christ's Church is located in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Our hope is to help people take their next step toward hope, healing, and community.

Some features open trusted external providers, including YouTube, Church Center, Google Maps, and RebelGive. Giving and forms are handled by those providers, not inside the app.
